Gator Gotcha privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

What we store

  • An anonymous account: created only when you finish a run and your score is submitted. It's a random ID stored in your browser; no email or password.
  • Your nickname: random (like "Duck1234") until you change it. Shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Your scores: score, eggs, time survived, the swamp seed, and the inputs of the run (used only to verify the score is genuine).
  • If you connect Facebook: your Facebook user ID, name and profile picture, used to show your name/avatar on the leaderboard and keep your scores across devices. We don't post to Facebook on your behalf.
  • On your device only: your local best score, mute setting, how many runs you've finished and when you last dismissed the install offer (browser storage).

Gameplay statistics

To learn how the game is played, it records a few gameplay events: title_view, run_start, run_end, retry, share, install, install_prompt_shown, install_prompt_outcome, update_applied, mode_select, level_end and deathcam_skip. Each carries game details only (such as time survived, eggs, score, close calls, times spotted, whether the app is installed, how the install offer was answered and the referring site's name), a random ID for the page visit, the game version, and your anonymous account ID if you have one. No other personal data is collected, and these statistics are never shown publicly or shared.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and the game is served by Vercel. We don't sell data, show ads or use tracking cookies.

Sharing

When you tap "Share on Facebook", Facebook receives a link to a public score card showing your nickname and score.

Deleting your data

Open a request at github.com/allandanos/gator-gotcha/issues with your nickname (or, if connected, the name on your Facebook account) and we'll delete your account and scores within 30 days. If you connected Facebook, you can also remove the app under Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.
